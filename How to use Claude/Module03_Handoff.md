## 4. Decisions and wording standards already established

**Tone and style.**
- Natural, practical, serious. Direct sentences.
- No marketing words ("premium," "world-class," "transformative").
- No motivational filler. No promises about jobs, income, or accreditation.
- Examples practical and recognizable for Kenyan learners (KES, Safaricom, KRA, iTax, Kenyan universities) but understandable globally. Cultural references should not require explanation but should not assume Western default.
- Technical terms defined the first time they appear.

**Structural conventions.**
- Eight-question checkpoint, not six.
- Pass threshold: 80%.
- Portfolio artifact filename pattern: `ModuleXX_ArtifactName_[YourName].pdf` or `.docx`.
- Each module ends with a forward-pointing transition into the next module.
- "Pause and check" sits between core lesson + worked examples and the knowledge-to-output task.

**Boilerplate to never include.**
- "Premium completion upgrade" header or content.
- "Self-learner success path" / "Evidence of completion" / "Automated/self-paced checkpoint recommendation" / "Minimal facilitator intervention points" five-block tail.
- "Validation and quality assurance checklist" / "Validation check."
- The 16× "Intervene only if the learner shows overtrust…" paragraph.
- "It is aligned to the same premium authoring standard."
- "Misconceptions to listen for during facilitation" — replace with a self-learner check.

**Self-learner readiness.**
- No "facilitator will introduce." Replace with "start by reading."
- No "ask the group." Use solo prompts.
- No "peer critique" without a documented solo alternative.

**Module 3 specific decisions.**
- Use the **T-C-C-F-A** model (Task, Context, Constraints, Format, Audience). The deeper R-T-C-C-O-R model belongs to Module 4.
- Worked examples must include real Kenyan context in at least one example (Worked example 3 — WhatsApp customer-delay message — is the natural place).
- Worked example 4 (performance review) is the place to show how AI invents specifics when not bounded by evidence.
