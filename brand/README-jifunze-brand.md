## Geometry (for anyone rebuilding the lockup)
On a 1600 × 900 artboard: icon 350 × 350 at (230, 275); wordmark and tagline both left-aligned
at x = 650; wordmark baseline y = 498.5; tagline baseline y = 579.8. The icon's centre and the
text block's optical centre both land on y = 450.

Social profile lockup (stacked): mark 331 px wide over the wordmark over the tagline, the whole
block optically centred in the 1080 square. Its furthest corner sits at 75 % of the circular-crop
radius, so nothing clips on any platform. The wordmark stays readable down to about a 96 px
circle; below that the tagline reads as texture, which is expected — use the plain icon
(`jifunze-icon.svg`) anywhere the avatar renders smaller than ~64 px.

## Clear space & minimum size
- Clear space around the lockup ≥ the height of the chevron mark.
- Full lockup with tagline: 180 px wide minimum. Below that use the no-tagline lockup.
- Below 64 px use the icon alone; below 48 px use `jifunze-appicon.svg`.

## Don'ts
No gradients, drop shadows, outlines or 3D effects. No recolouring outside the palette above.
Do not stretch, rotate, crop or re-space the lockup.
