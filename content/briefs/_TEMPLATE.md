# Course Brief Template

**How to use this template.** Copy this file to `content/briefs/<slug>.md` (where `<slug>` matches the eventual course directory). Fill in every section. The brief is the single most important input to AI-assisted authoring — the difference between a strong course and a generic one happens here.

Keep this brief tight: 1–2 pages when filled in. The AI authoring system reads it verbatim.

---

## 1. Slug and title

- **Slug:** `your-course-slug` (kebab-case, will become the URL segment)
- **Title:** `Your Course Title`
- **Subtitle (one line):** `Short subtitle for catalog cards`
- **School:** one of `ai_digital | business_growth | career_intellect | leadership_learning | mathematics | sciences | specialization`
- **Tier:** one of `flagship | standalone | library | free_starter`
- **Level range:** e.g. `Beginner to Intermediate`
- **Duration label:** e.g. `4–6 hours`
