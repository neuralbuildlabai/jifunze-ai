## Producer-side global notes

**Slide transitions.** All transitions are simple cross-fade (200–300ms). No fancy animations between slides — they distract from instructional content.

**Cursor visibility.** Use a slightly enlarged cursor with a subtle yellow halo so viewers can follow the pointer when you spotlight elements. Most screen recorders have this option.

**Lower-thirds for techniques.** Optional but recommended: as each new technique or principle is introduced, show a 2–3 second lower-third with the technique name in the deck's purple. Helps with skimming and rewatch.

**Slide 9 (8 techniques grid) is dense.** It's intentional — it's a map slide. Don't dwell. ~40 seconds is the right time on screen. If your narrator reads it slowly, animate each card brightening as they read it.

**Slide 22 (Thank You) is also dense** with the next-steps list. Linger here — this is the slide a learner is most likely to pause and reread. 2 minutes of voice over is appropriate.

**No slide needs to be rewritten before recording.** Densest slides (9, 22) are intentional and supported by the voiceover pacing.

**Title cards (Session N — …)** are inserted in the editor between sessions, not in the deck. See `session-plan.md` for title-card text.
